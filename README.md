# Bike-Sharing-Demand-Analysis
Bike-sharing demand analysis using SQL to explore rental patterns, seasonal trends, weather conditions, and user behavior.

**Project Overview**

This project analyzes bike-sharing data using SQL to understand rental patterns, customer demand, and the factors associated with bicycle usage.

The analysis focuses on how bike rentals change over time and how weather conditions, seasons, and other recorded variables relate to rental demand.

The goal is to identify patterns in bike usage, understand periods of high and low demand, and present findings that can help support operational planning and resource allocation.

**Background**

Bike-sharing systems are a newer generation of traditional bicycle rental services. They automate the process of membership, rental, and bicycle return, allowing users to rent a bicycle from one location and return it to another.

These systems play an important role in urban transportation and are relevant to traffic management, environmental sustainability, and public health.

Bike-sharing systems also generate useful data for research and analysis. Unlike some traditional transportation services, bike-sharing systems can record details such as rental duration, departure and arrival locations, and usage patterns.

This makes bike-sharing data useful for understanding mobility within a city. By monitoring rental activity, it may be possible to identify transportation patterns and changes in urban activity.

**Project Objectives**

This project aims to:

- Analyze overall bike rental demand.
- Identify hourly, daily, monthly, and seasonal rental patterns.
- Examine differences in rental activity between working days, weekends, and holidays.
- Explore the relationship between weather conditions and bike rental demand.
- Compare rental behavior between casual and registered users.
- Identify periods of high and low bicycle usage.
- Examine how rental demand changes throughout the year.
- Develop recommendations that may help improve bicycle availability and operational planning.
